## Cautions when comparing clean‑AES and baseline scores

- **Different artifacts entirely.** Clean AES currently has only a *functional testbench* (5 PASS), not a detector run; the recovered baseline has a *detector* run. These are not comparable outputs. The clean‑AES detector result is pending — do not invent it or benchmark absent numbers against the baseline.
- **No design equivalence.** The 128‑bit AES reference and the 32‑bit‑interface baseline are distinct; matching port width or a matching ZIP SHA does not establish the same algorithm. Do not substitute one for the other.
- **Scores are not security guarantees.** The baseline CLEAN votes classify a coarse fallback graph after extraction failed; a CLEAN vote is not proof of Trojan‑freedom, and false‑negative/calibration behavior on this netlist is unvalidated. Mean/max/min P(Trojan) are descriptive aggregates, not probabilities of compromise.
- Any future clean‑AES detector numbers must carry the same preprocessing/checkpoint‑identity/distribution caveats.
