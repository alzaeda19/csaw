PORTABILITY AND HONEST COVERAGE
Copied reports, scripts and historical records retain their original absolute paths and original bytes. SOURCE_COPY_RECEIPTS.json supplies relocation to this bundle. Historical manifests may describe a larger original corpus; missing files from those historic manifests are not claimed as packaged here. PACKAGE_HASHES.json is the authority for this bundle's contents.

This is a portable evidence subset, not a complete stand-alone EDA environment. Toolchains, large waveforms, full trace CSVs and solver intermediates remain in the preserved source workspaces. Historical runners may require their original environment and paths. The clean AES standalone runner has its own README. Nothing is automatically executed when this archive is opened.

All copied evidence must remain distinguishable from newly authored briefs and recovery summaries. Hashes establish byte consistency, not independent completeness or instructor acceptance. No project code, tests, detector inference, notebook or board operation was run while organizing this package. No source/repository files were changed; no published solutions were consulted.
